Key features

Five tabs. One daily ritual.

The mic sits in the centre of the tab bar because sharing your day is the signature act. Everything else feeds it or rewards it.

🎙
Signature

Mann Darpan: speak your day, get your shloka

Hold the mic. Talk in Hindi, English or Hinglish about a bad review, a breakup, exam panic, or a win you want to stay grounded about. In about 4 seconds you hear the one shloka that fits, why it fits, and three small things to do in the next 24 hours.

↑

Shloka Feed

Full-screen vertical swipe through all 700 verses. Sanskrit, pronunciation, Hindi and English meaning, chant audio, illustrated parchment backgrounds.

◎

Tap to go deeper

Three tabs on every card: Shabdarth (word by word), Katha (who said it to whom, and why), Jeevan (what it means for your Monday).

✓

Checkpoint cards

Every 7 swipes, one quick quiz or reflection card. This is what turns a scroll into learning and where most feed points come from.

◉

Moksh Points

Earn for reading, reflecting, finishing lessons and keeping promises. Lose for speed-scrolling and broken commitments, never for a bad mood.

✦

Daily Sankalp

One small promise from today's lesson ("speak calmly at home tonight"). Evening check-in: done, partly, not today. Honesty always pays.

🛕

Gita Yatra

18 chapters as a winding path, like the 18 days of Kurukshetra. Short lessons, quizzes, chapter badges, seasonal challenges on Gita Jayanti.

👥

Sangha

Weekly leagues of 30, family groups with a shared streak flame, and a Sakha (friend) you can nudge when they miss a day.

↗

Share cards

Auto-designed parchment cards for Instagram Story and WhatsApp Status. The main growth loop, with a deep link back to the verse.

How a shloka reads inside the app

Sanskrit, said simply

Every verse gets the original, an English-spelled pronunciation you can actually say out loud, a plain meaning, and one real-life situation where it applies.

Adhyay 2 · Shloka 47 · Karma Yoga
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥
Kar-man-ye vaa-dhi-kaa-ras-te · maa pha-le-shu ka-daa-cha-na
maa kar-ma-pha-la he-tur bhoor · maa te san-go'stva a-kar-ma-ni
तुम्हारा अधिकार सिर्फ़ कर्म पर है, उसके फल पर कभी नहीं।
In simple words
Do the work in front of you with full effort. The result is not fully in your hands, so don't tie your peace to it. And don't use that as an excuse to stop working.
Katha · who is speaking
Krishna to Arjuna, on the battlefield, right after Arjuna says he'd rather not fight than face the outcome.
Real life: You submitted the assignment, pitched the client, sat the interview. The nervous refreshing of your inbox is the "phala" part. The shloka says: you've done your bit, now put the phone down.
Adhyay 3 · Shloka 21 · Karma Yoga
यद्यदाचरति श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो जनः।
स यत्प्रमाणं कुरुते लोकस्तदनुवर्तते॥
Yad-yad aa-cha-ra-ti shresh-thas · tat-tad e-ve-ta-ro ja-nah
sa yat pra-maa-nam ku-ru-te · lo-kas tad a-nu-var-ta-te
श्रेष्ठ व्यक्ति जैसा करता है, बाकी लोग वैसा ही करते हैं।
In simple words
People copy what leaders do, not what they say. Whatever standard you set, others around you follow it.
Katha
Krishna points to King Janaka, who kept doing his duty after enlightenment, because the kingdom was watching.
Real life: Your younger cousin copies how you treat your phone at dinner, how you talk to Mummy, how you handle a loss. You are already someone's "shreshtha". Pick one habit today you'd be proud to see copied.
Adhyay 6 · Shloka 5 · Dhyana Yoga
उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत्।
आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः॥
Ud-dha-red aat-ma-naat-maa-nam · naat-maa-nam a-va-saa-da-yet
aat-mai-va hyaat-ma-no ban-dhur · aat-mai-va ri-pur aat-ma-nah
अपने आप को खुद उठाओ, खुद को गिरने मत दो। तुम ही अपने मित्र हो, तुम ही अपने शत्रु।
In simple words
Lift yourself up with your own mind. Don't let yourself sink. The same mind can be your best friend or your worst enemy, depending on how you train it.
Katha
Opening of the chapter on meditation. Before any technique, Krishna says the first job is to stop being your own saboteur.
Real life: 1 am, scrolling, the voice says "everyone is ahead of you". That voice is the ripu (enemy). The bandhu (friend) is the one that says "close the app, sleep, 5 shlokas tomorrow". Same mind. You choose which one gets the mic.
Adhyay 2 · Shloka 48 · Sankhya Yoga
योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनञ्जय।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते॥
Yo-gas-thah ku-ru kar-maa-ni · san-gam tyak-tvaa dha-nan-ja-ya
sid-dhya-sid-dhyoh sa-mo bhoot-vaa · sa-mat-vam yo-ga uch-ya-te
सफलता और असफलता में एक जैसा रहना, यही योग है।
In simple words
Stay steady whether it works out or not. That evenness of mind is what "yoga" actually means.
Why it's in the app
This is the verse Mann Darpan serves on good days. Joy gets celebrated, then gently grounded, so the next bad day doesn't crash as hard.
Real life: You cleared the DSA round. Enjoy it fully tonight. Then remember the same you who cleared it also failed two before. Samatvam means the win doesn't become your identity any more than the losses did.

Principle

Server-authoritative points

Client shows optimistic MP. The server validates every event, applies daily caps and anti-gaming rules, and writes an append-only ledger with a rule version. Every point is auditable.

Principle

Retrieval before generation

Hybrid score: 0.5 tag overlap + 0.4 vector similarity + 0.1 freshness, reranked to top 3. Target: top-1 accepted by a scholar on 80% of a 500-entry gold set, top-3 on 95%.

Cost

₹2.6 to 3.4 per voice session

STT ₹0.50, tagging ₹0.10, lesson ₹0.50 to 1.20, rerank ₹0.05, TTS ₹1.50. Text-first answers halve it. That is why Mann Darpan, not the feed, is the paywall lever.
